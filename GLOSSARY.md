# Tripwise

A group trip planner: friends share one itinerary, vote on ideas, keep their bookings in one place and split costs.

## Accounts

**Account**:
A person's way into Tripwise — an email address and a password. One account can belong to many trips.
_Avoid_: User (in product copy), login, profile

**Demo account**:
The single shared account any visitor can explore with, without signing up. Its password can never be reset or changed.
_Avoid_: Test account, guest account

**Password reset**:
Replacing a forgotten password through a reset link sent to the account's email address, without knowing the old one. Completing it signs the account out everywhere else.
_Avoid_: Password recovery, forgot password (except as the link label)

**Reset link**:
The one-time link in a password-reset email. It stops working after 30 minutes, or as soon as the password changes.
_Avoid_: Reset token (in product copy), magic link
